2.1. Preparation of samples
The diagram of the lab-scale MBR used in this study is presented in
Supplementary information. The MBR system primarily consisted of the
sludge suspension and a membrane unit. The unit was comprised of
four ﬂat-sheet PVDF membrane elements (SINAP Co. Ltd., Shanghai)
with normalized pore size of 0.10 µm. In this study, the particulate
sludge in the sludge suspension sampled in the steady operation stage of
MBR was used as typical foulants. The foulant samples for contact angle
measurements were prepared by ﬁltration of sludge suspension to form
a cake layer followed by fastening the cake layer within two glass slides
and drying them in a desiccator (40 °C) for 24 h. Accordingly, the
membrane samples for contact angle measurements were prepared by
similar procedure.
