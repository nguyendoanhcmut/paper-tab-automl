4. Conclusions
An RBF network was proposed to predict the interfacial interactions
with randomly rough membrane surface related with membrane
fouling. For training the network, the interactions with rough mem-
brane surface were also quantiﬁed by the advanced XDLVO approach.
The computing time consumption for interaction prediction by the RBF
network was only about 1/50 of that by the advance XDLVO approach
under same conditions, indicating high eﬃciency of the RBF ANN
method. Meanwhile, the RBF networks trained by no less than 10
