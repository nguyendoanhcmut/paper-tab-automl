2.2. Analytical methods
Contact angles of the three probe liquids on the sludge layer and the
membrane surface were tested by using a contact angle meter (Kino,
USA) according to the dropping method. Surface zeta potential of the
particulate sludge in the sludge suspension was measured by using a
Malvern Zetasizer Nano ZS instrument. In contrast, zeta potential of the
membrane surface was measured using a NanoBrook 90Plus Zeta
Particle Size Analyzer. Images of the original PVDF membrane surface
were captured by an atomic force microscopy (AFM) (NT-MDT, USA).
The statistical height and root mean square roughness of the AFM
images were calculated by the software NanoScope analysis V1.80.
