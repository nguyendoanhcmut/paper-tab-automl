2.4. Predictive control system based on LR modelling
An LR-based predictive control strategy was developed for the MPBR using four manipulated operational variables: solids retention 
time (SRT), hydraulic retention time (HRT), aeration flow rate (Fair), and ammonium nitrogen loading rate (NLR). Two empirical 
relationships were incorporated as operational constraints: OD680 = f (SRT) (Fig. S3) and the dependence of NLR on HRT through the 
influent ammonium concentration (Equation (5)).

The implementation strategy was defined as follows: i) for high NH4– Neff concentrations, Fair, HRT, and SRT were maximized,

tively selected to ensure that the resulting SRT and HRT values remained within the applicability ranges of the LR models. The optimal 
selection of operating parameters incorporated the interdependent relationships NLR = f (HRT) (Equation (5)) and OD680 = f(SRT)
(Fig. S3). Incorporating these relationships required the iterative identification of operating parameters. In this approach, when SRT 
and HRT were adjusted, the corresponding OD680 and NLR values were proportionally modified from their current states.
