2. Methodology
The study followed a four-step framework (Fig. 1): Step 1, collection and pre-processing of long-term MPBR operational data; Step 
2, development and validation of the LR models for probabilistic classification of NH4-Neff; Step 3, local and global sensitivity analyses 
to identify influential variables and their interactions; and Step 4, in-silico evaluation of a multivariable supervisory control strategy to 
improve ammonium compliance and reclaimed-water production across four independent measurement campaigns (Table 1).
