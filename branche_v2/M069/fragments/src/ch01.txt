A B S T R A C T
This study presents a data-driven approach for the water management sector, utilizing a logistic 
regression (LR) framework to predict effluent ammonium nitrogen (NH4-Neff) concentrations and 
assess water reuse suitability. The system under investigation is an outdoor membrane photo­
bioreactor (MPBR) operating as a tertiary treatment step for nutrient removal from the effluent of 
an anaerobic membrane bioreactor (AnMBR), which is responsible for primary organic matter 
removal from municipal wastewater. The proposed framework estimates the likelihood of 
compliance with stringent Italian reuse regulations (NH4-Neff < 1.6 mg N/L). Utilizing 1.5 years 
of continuous outdoor operational data, eight binary LR models were developed. The input 
variables are explicitly defined and encompass operational parameters, solids retention time 
(SRT), hydraulic retention time (HRT), aeration flowrate (Fair), and nitrogen loading rate (NLR), 
as well as environmental and biological factors, including light availability, temperature, dis­
solved oxygen, and microalgal biomass indicators. An advanced control strategy was simulated to 
optimize the four operational parameters simultaneously. In-silico projections demonstrated 
significant practical advantages over fixed operating conditions: enhanced NH4-Neff removal ef­
ficiency (12–50% reduction), decreased aeration requirements (32–38% reduction in Fair), and 
increased production of compliant reclaimed water (up to 750 L per m3 of treated wastewater). 
The proposed framework could therefore support supervisory decision-making and potential 
integration with existing SCADA systems while retaining a transparent analytical structure.
