2.5. Cluster analysis
To complement the individual-variable interpretation provided by the local sensitivity analysis, a multivariate cluster analysis was 
conducted using the experimental values of HRT, SRT, NLR, PLR, PAR, PAR, T, T, OD680, DO, DO, and NH4–N. Before clustering, the 
variables were standardized to prevent differences in their numerical scales from influencing the analysis. The observations were 
classified into four clusters using K – means. The number of clusters was selected based on Ward's algorithm for minimizing variance in 
clusters and the Euclidean distance measure. Mean cluster characteristics and standardized profiles are presented in Table S13 and 
Fig. S4, respectively.
