3.4.2. Experimental MPBR data
Experimental data showed that, under fixed HRT and SRT conditions, the pilot-scale MPBR frequently exceeded the Italian reuse 
limit for effluent ammonium (NH4-Neff). During Periods 1 and 2, compliance was experimentally observed for only approximately 
three days, corresponding to about 8% of the operating time (Fig. 6a and b). Period 4 exhibited the highest compliance, with the reuse 
standard met for nearly 60 of the 89 operating days (Fig. 6d). However, between days 70 and 89, the measured NH4-Neff concentration 
increased sharply and reached the highest experimentally observed values.
