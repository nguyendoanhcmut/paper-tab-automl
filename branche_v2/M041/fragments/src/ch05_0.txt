Modeling description
A combination of RSM, NSGA-II, and ANN modeling techniques was used to accurately estimate the 
effectiveness of removing ammonia nitrogen and phosphate content in a microalgae membrane bioreactor. 
Combining RSM, NSGA-II, and ANN modeling provides a robust and flexible way to predict and improve 
phosphate concentration, removal efficiency, and ammonia nitrogen removal in a microalgae membrane 
bioreactor. This technique may change how wastewater treatment systems that use microalgae are designed and 
operated, leading to increased cost-effectiveness, sustainability, and efficiency.
