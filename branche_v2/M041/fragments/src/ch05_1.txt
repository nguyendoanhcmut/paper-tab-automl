Data acquisition and preprocessing
The experimental dataset for this study was adopted from the work of Ding et al.23, focusing on the treatment of 
mariculture wastewater in a microalgae membrane bioreactor (MBR). This study functions as a computational 
modeling reanalysis to extract optimization insights that were not explored in the original experimental study. 
The dataset consists of 44 distinct experimental data points. The input variables utilized are Time (days), pH, and 
Total Organic Carbon (TOC, mg/L), while the output response variables are Ammonia Nitrogen concentration 
(mg/L), Phosphate concentration (mg/L), and their respective Removal Efficiencies (%).
To ensure efficient convergence during the ANN training process, all input and output data were normalized 
to a range of [0, 1] using the Min-Max normalization technique, defined as:

Where Xnorm is the normalized value, Xi is the actual value, and Xmin and Xmax are the minimum and maximum 
values of the dataset, respectively. For the ANN modeling, the 44 data points were randomly partitioned into 
three subsets: 70% for training to adjust the network weights, 15% for validation to prevent overfitting, and 15% 
for testing to evaluate the model’s generalization capability on unseen data.
