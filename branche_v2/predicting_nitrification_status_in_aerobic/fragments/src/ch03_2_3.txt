2.2.3. Data preprocessing
From 128 datasets collected over 235 days from dual MBRs, eight incomplete entries were excluded. Biocarriers were added to one 
reactor in Phase 3 to enable denitrification (Fig. 1), altering parameter relationships. To evaluate cross-scenario applicability, 23 data 
groups from Phase 3 were used as an independent test group for models trained exclusively on 97 no-biocarrier data groups (activated 
sludge only). The 97 data groups were utilized for training, validation, and testing of the model’s performance in predicting nitrifi­
cation with activated sludge only. This approach assessed performance under altered operational conditions. Given inherent influent 
variability in onsite greywater systems, outliers were intentionally retained to evaluate model robustness against operational fluc­
tuations, reflecting real-world dynamics.
