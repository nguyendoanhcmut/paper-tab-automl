D.2
Comparison with Other Feature
Importance Metrics
Table 9 shows the consistency of feature impor-
tance rankings across Pearson, SHAP, mutual in-
formation and Spearman, measured by Kendall’s
τ. We select Spearman as the primary evaluation
metric because it achieved the highest consistency
score (τ = 0.65) among all candidates, indicat-
ing that its ranking of feature importance aligns
most robustly with others. This superior agreement
suggests that Spearman provides the most reliable
assessment of feature relevance in our context.
