SymboLLM-FE demonstrates superior overall
performance, outperforming all compared Genetic
Programming variants on the majority of datasets.
It exhibits particularly significant gains in regres-
sion tasks and secures top rankings in most classifi-
cation benchmarks, remaining highly competitive
even in the single instance where a GP variant holds
a marginal lead. Moreover, SymboLLM-FE dis-
plays greater stability and robustness across diverse
data distributions, avoiding the performance volatil-
ity often observed in evolutionary search methods.
These results confirm that SymboLLM-FE not only
matches but frequently exceeds the predictive ca-
pabilities of traditional and advanced GP methods,
while offering the additional advantages of LLM-
driven semantic guidance and faster convergence.
