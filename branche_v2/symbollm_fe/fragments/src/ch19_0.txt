Case Study
We present eight case studies drawn from two
datasets, the Spaceship Titanic dataset and the
CMC dataset, that illustrate how SymboLLM-FE
transforms opaque symbolic regression expressions
into semantically interpretable features. Each case
follows a four-part structure: the raw symbolic re-
gression formula with its performance metric, the
interpretability challenge posed by the formula, the
LLM-generated feature with its accompanying ra-
tionale, and the domain-grounded semantic inter-
pretation that results from this augmentation.
