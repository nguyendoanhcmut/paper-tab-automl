The dataset was first preprocessed to ensure consistency and us­
ability for ML models. After data cleaning and preprocessing, a total of 
2191 observations remained and were used for the ML analysis. All 
feature and target variables were checked for formatting issues, such as 
commas or whitespace in numeric fields, which were removed to allow 
for conversion into floating-point numbers. Any missing or invalid 
values were imputed using the mean strategy through the SimpleIm­
puter function, thereby preventing data loss while maintaining statisti­
cal consistency [35,36]. To improve comparability across features with 
different scales and units, standardization was applied using the 
StandardScaler, which transforms the features into zero mean and unit 
variance. This normalization step ensures that each variable contributes 
equally to model training and avoids bias towards features with larger 
numerical ranges.
The operating parameters used in the ML modeling of the MBR 
system are provided in Table 1. The selection of these 18 feature vari­
ables was further justified by their direct relevance to the study's core 
research objectives. Temperature parameters across the anaerobic, 
anoxic, and aerobic zones were specifically included to capture seasonal 
suppression of nitrifying activity, directly addressing the low- 
temperature nitrification limitation identified as a key research gap 
[37,38]. Oxidation-reduction potential (ORP) and DO measurements 
across all stages were designated to quantify the redox conditions gov­
erning denitrification efficiency and PAO metabolism, thereby enabling 
investigation of the oxygen imbalance problem [39,40]. Mixed liquor 
volatile suspended solids (MLVSS) were incorporated alongside MLSS to 
distinguish active microbial biomass from inert solids, providing a more 
accurate indicator of the biological potential for nutrient removal. 
Sludge volume index (SVI) was included to monitor sludge settleability, 
as poor settling can lead to biomass washout and deteriorated effluent 
quality in MBR systems [41–43]. The return sludge flow rate (Sludge-R) 
was specifically selected because it controls the recycle of nitrate-rich 
mixed liquor to the anoxic zone, directly influencing denitrification 
capacity. By systematically including parameters that capture both 
environmental conditions and biological state variables, the feature set 
ensures that the ML models learn the underlying process mechanisms 
rather than merely fitting correlations, enhancing model interpretability 
and enabling meaningful SHAP analysis of each innovation's 
contribution.
