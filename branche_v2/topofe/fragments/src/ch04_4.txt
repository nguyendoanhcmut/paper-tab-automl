Parallelism.
Island evaluations are mutually independent, so all M islands execute in parallel, reducing wall-clock
time per generation from O(n·Ceval) to O(maxi ni ·Ceval) and yielding an approximate k-fold speedup under balanced
partitioning.

Sample Efficiency.
Saturation-triggered transfer concentrates oracle evaluations on high-utility regions of P, reducing
the number of generations T required to converge relative to single-population search. TOPOFE thus achieves
comparable asymptotic complexity to the baseline while improving both wall-clock efficiency through parallelism and
convergence speed through principled cross-family exploration.
