To better understand the characteristics of data collected from the anammox-based processes, the box plots, 
EDA, and PPMC were utilized to analyze collected data (Fig. 2). There were 2940 samples collected from 28 
studies, which investigated different types of anammox-based processes and applied different operation 
conditions. Box plots illustrated the distribution of data from different groups. Outliers in all groups indicated 
occasional significant deviations among different experiments. As shown in Fig. 2(a), the sequencing batch 
experiments (median = 78.18%) showed a higher TIN removal efficiency than the continuous flow experiments 
(median = 74.81%). Meanwhile, more stable NLR, NiRR, NARR, and NRR were observed in the sequencing batch 
experiments. Extremely high NLR (2.91–7.55 kg N/m3/d), NiRR (0.33–2.53 kg N/m3/d), NARR (0.02–3.92 kg N/
m3/d) and NRR (2.63–6.55 kg N/m3/d) were found in Du et al. (2016)35 and Wang et al. (2024b)36 due to their 
high influent nitrogen concentration and short HRT in the UASB and expanded granular sludge bed reactor 
operating under continuous flow. This difference was also obtained between the floc experiments and aggregate 
experiments (Fig. 2(b)). The high NLR in continuous flow reactors operated by aggregates demonstrated that 
this combination had great potential to treat wastewater with high levels of nitrogen37,38.
For experiments using synthetic wastewater and municipal wastewater, the effluent properties were similar 
(Fig. S1(a)). However, the experiments using municipal wastewater had more stable NLR, NiRR, NARR, and 
NRR (Fig. S1(b)), suggesting that influent properties of municipal were analogous across different studies39–42. 
Instead, the synthetic wastewater utilized in different studies had a larger range of influent COD, NH4
+-N 
and NO3
−-N concentrations35,36,43,44. To further analyze the differences in influent properties of synthetic and 
municipal wastewater, the distribution of influent properties was visualized by the EDA approach (Fig. 2(c) 
and (d)). Data points in larger regions were observed in the C/N versus influent NH4
+-N, C/N versus influent 
TIN, influent COD versus influent NH4
+-N, and influent COD versus influent TIN in Fig. 2(d), indicating 
that the influent variables of synthetic wastewater were more concentrated and had less flocculation than that 
of municipal wastewater. In reality, the concentrations of pollutants in real wastewater normally fluctuate 
greatly45,46. Therefore, the experiments using synthetic wastewater need to further take into account the real 
composition and fluctuation of the municipal wastewater.
Notably, the data from different anammox-based process types (i.e., PNA, PDA, and PNA combined with 
PDA) presented high differences (Fig. S2). The PNA and PNA combined with PDA experiments had higher TIN 
removal efficiency than the PDA experiments (Fig. S2(a)). In all three types of anammox-based processes, the 
effluent NH4
+-N, effluent NO2
−-N, and effluent TIN were positively correlated to the influent NH4
+-N, influent 
TIN, and NLR, whereas the strongest positive correlation (0.55–0.95) was found in the PNA experiments 
(Fig. 2(e), (f) and (g)). Unlike PDA processes, the realization of PNA processes requires inhibition of nitrite-
oxidizing bacteria (NOB) activity and enhancement of ammonia-oxidizing bacteria (AOB) activity47. The NOB 
and AOB activity have been found to be largely affected by the substrate concentrations (e.g., NO2
−-N, NH4
+-N, 
and COD)48,49. This may be the reason for the stronger correlation between influent and effluent variables in 
PNA experiments.
Moreover, the NiRR, NARR, and NRR had strong correlations with the variables involved in their calculations 
(Text S1). Meanwhile, the influent NH4
+-N and influent TIN had very similar pairwise relationships with other 
variables because NH4
+-N was extremely high in both synthetic and municipal wastewater. For real municipal 
wastewater, the NH4
+-N normally accounts for more than 90% of influent TIN42,50.

pairwise relationships were found between the influent type and dominant anammox bacteria, influent NO3
−-N 
as well as influent TIN. This correlation further highlighted the differences between synthetic wastewater and 
municipal wastewater. Except for influent NH4
+-N and influent TIN, no other strong linear relationship was
