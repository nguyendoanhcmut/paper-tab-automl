To further explore the numerical dependence of NH4
+-N removal, the 1D PDP of important numerical input 
variables are illustrated (Fig. S4). The response of effluent NH4

later period of anammox-based experiments14,58,59. Meanwhile, the 1D PDP results revealed an appropriate range 
of C/N (2.72–6.32), in which lower effluent NH4
+-N concentrations were achieved compared with C/N < 2.72

NH4

For instance, Miao et al. (2018)60 reported that the removal efficiency of NH4
+-N in the anammox process 
increased progressively as the C/N ratio rose from 1.1 to 2.5.

These results suggested that municipal wastewater with nitrogen concentrations below these threshold values 
may be more suitable to be treated by the anammox-based nitrogen removal processes. Actually, the influent 
NH4

ensured the high contribution of anammox process for municipal wastewater.
