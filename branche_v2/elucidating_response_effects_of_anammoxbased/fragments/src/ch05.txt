This study explored the response effects of important effluent variables to operation conditions and influent 
characteristics involved in the anammox-based nitrogen removal processes through the big data analysis and 
AutoML algorithm. Analysis of big data collected from the literature presented that the continuous flow reactors 
with anammox aggregates could address higher nitrogen loadings in municipal wastewater. High accuracy of 
the candidate models generated by the AutoML algorithm for these important output variables was achieved 
for data collected from both literature and unseen experiments. Moreover, the interpretable analysis of the 
optimal models clarified the 1D and 2D response effects between input and output variables. The appropriate 
ranges of key characteristics such as C/N, influent NH4
+-N, influent TIN, influent COD, HRT, and NLR were 
identified. These findings would deepen the understanding and support the practical operation of anammox-
based processes for treating municipal wastewater.
