Baselines
We evaluated LLM-FE against state-of-the-art feature engineering approaches, including OpenFE (Zhang
et al., 2023) and AutoFeat (Horn et al., 2020), as well as LLM-based methods CAAFE (Hollmann et al., 2024),
FeatLLM (Han et al., 2024), and OCTree (Nam et al., 2024). We used XGBoost as the default tabular data
prediction model in comparison with baselines and employed GPT-3.5-Turbo as the default LLM backbone
for all LLM-based methods (Tables 2 and 3). To ensure a fair comparison, all LLM-based approaches
operate under a fixed budget of 20 LLM samples with no early stopping or convergence criterion; the final
reported result corresponds to the best-scoring program discovered within this budget. Appendix B.2 contains
additional implementation details.
