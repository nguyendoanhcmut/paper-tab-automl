2.3  Analytical Methods
Physicochemical parameters including temperature, pH, 
COD, turbidity, TSS, TDS, VSS, 2,4-DCP concentration 
and electrical conductivity (EC) were monitored through­
out the study. COD and phenol concentrations were deter­
mined using the closed reflux colorimetric method (APHA 
5220D) and direct photometric method (APHA 5530D), 
respectively, with a DR 6000 spectrophotometer (Hach, 
Germany). Turbidity was measured using the nephelometric 
method with a Hach 2100AN turbidimeter..

pH was adjusted using 0.1 N H₂SO₄ and 0.1 N NaOH 
and measured with a multi-parameter analyser (CON­
SORT C831, Belgium). TSS, TDS, and VSS were anal­
ysed in accordance with APHA Standard Methods 2540 C, 
2540D, and 2540E. For TSS, 25 mL samples were filtered 
through pre-weighed Whatman GF/C filters (UK), dried at 
103–105 °C for 24 h (Memmert oven), cooled in a desicca­
tor, and reweighed. TDS was determined by evaporating 25 
mL samples at 180 °C until a constant weight was achieved. 
VSS was calculated by igniting the TSS filters at 550 °C 
for 30 min in a muffle furnace (Nabertherm, Germany), fol­
lowed by cooling and reweighing. All measurements were 
conducted in triplicate, and average values were reported.
