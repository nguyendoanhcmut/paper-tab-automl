In addition, the use of multivariate imputation approaches such as MICE is supported by previous studies on environ­
mental and water-quality datasets, which have shown that multivariate methods generally outperform simple 
interpolation-based techniques when handling mixed-type and process-driven missing data (Dixneuf et al. 2021). Similar 
findings have also been reported in water-quality monitoring studies, indicating that multivariate approaches, including 
MICE and tree-based methods, are more suitable for reconstructing longer-term or structurally missing segments than 
simple interpolation methods (Wang et al. 2024). Overall, the MICE-based imputation procedure maintained the general
