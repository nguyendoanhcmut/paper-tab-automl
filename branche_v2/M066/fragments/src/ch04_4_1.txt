3.4.1. Data characteristics and correlation analysis
Distinct variability patterns among operational parameters and those 
of water quality were identified using descriptive statistics (Table S2). 
Influent COD had moderate variability with 10684 mg/L and a CV of 
26.2%, indicating organic loading conditions fluctuated greatly. Effluent 
COD had a substantially greater relative CV of 90.6%, indicating wide- 
ranging treatment performance under different operational phases, 
particularly during shock loading. The conditions within the anaerobic 
system remained stable as demonstrated by a consistent negative ORP 
(−510 to −378 mV) and low CV (6.1%) indicating stable reducing 
conditions exist in the reactor. In contrast, TMP exhibited a high degree 
of variability CV (82%) indicating that membrane fouling develops non- 
linearly and is dependent upon the phase duration which makes pre­
dictive modelling very difficult [10]. The increase in effluent NH4
