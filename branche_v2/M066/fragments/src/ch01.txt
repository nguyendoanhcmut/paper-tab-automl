A B S T R A C T
The application of machine learning (ML) offers significant advantages in predicting critical performance metrics 
during wastewater treatment, enabling optimized operational control and mitigating challenges such as mem­
brane fouling. This study employed ML methods to evaluate COD removal and membrane fouling in a mesophilic 
anaerobic membrane bioreactor (AnMBR) for transfer station landfill leachate treatment operated for 120 days. 
The system achieved ≥80% COD removal at organic loading rate (OLR) of 6.4 kg COD/m³ ⋅d and hydraulic 
retention time (HRT) of 6.5 days. Artificial neural networks (ANN), random forest (RF), and extreme gradient 
boosting (XGBoost) were developed to predict effluent COD and transmembrane pressure (TMP). RF achieved the 
strongest predictive performance for both effluent COD (R² = 0.96; RMSE = 331.50 mg/L) and TMP (R² = 0.88; 
RMSE = 3.86 kPa). SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) analysis identified OLR, HRT and oxidation reduction 
potential (ORP) as the main model-associated predictors for effluent COD, while effluent NH4
+-N, effluent pH and 
ORP contributed most strongly to TMP prediction. These results show that integrating AnMBR operation with 
interpretable ML-based predictive modelling can provide useful diagnostic insights for optimizing leachate 
treatment performance and membrane fouling management.
