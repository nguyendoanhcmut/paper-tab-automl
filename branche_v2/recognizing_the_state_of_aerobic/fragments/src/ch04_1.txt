The effectiveness of the detector is commonly assessed through the 
mean average precision (mAP) across various classes. In this study, the 
metric was derived from ranking detection scores specific to each class 
(Everingham et al., 2010), and the IoU measured the accuracy of object 
detection in a particular dataset, which was the ratio of the predicted 
frames to the actual frames in object detection. The mAP50 set IoU to 
0.5, calculated AP for each class of images, and then averaged them 
across all classes, therefore, mAP50-95 could be understood as an 
average value calculated at the intervals of 0.05 from 0.5 to 0.95 at 
different IoUs. With an increase in iterations, there was a gradual 
improvement in precision, recall, mAP50, and mAP50-95 indicators, 
which initially fluctuated but eventually approached a plateau close to 1 
(Fig. 3(g) and (h)). The final model achieved an mAP50 score of 0.985 
(Fig. 3(i)), while the mAP50-95 reached 0.837 (Fig. 3(j)). In reality,
