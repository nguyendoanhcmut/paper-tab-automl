For a more comprehensive understanding of the model’s perfor­
mance, it is crucial to load the test set in order to evaluate the effec­
tiveness of the trained model. Hence, this study employed various 
evaluation indexes, including the confusion matrix, F1-score, precision 
and recall curve for analysis, in which, the confusion matrix, consisting 
of a 5 × 5 grid that included a background class, visually represented the 
relationship between actual and predicted values across different cate­
gories, together with the instances of mis-classification and false posi­
tives. Precision refers to the predictive accuracy in identifying the 
positive samples correctly, whereas recall measures how well positive 
samples are identified from the original dataset. The F1-score, ranging 
from 0 to 1, serves as a balanced measure by combining both precision 
and recall into one metric, with the optimal performance indicated by 1, 
and the least effective by 0.
