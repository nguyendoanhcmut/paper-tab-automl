The study employed a previously reported MBR system with internal 
circulation as the physical model (Dai et al., 2020), in which, a 
multi-habitat environment was created through accumulated biomass, 
facilitated by the hydrodynamic conditions of internal circulation. The 
activated sludge used for inoculation was obtained from the secondary 
sedimentation tank of a local municipal wastewater treatment plant 
located in Haizhu District, Guangzhou, China (Biological Island Sewage 
Treatment Plant). The initial concentration of mixed liquor suspended 
solids (MLSS) was maintained at approximately 3000 mg/L. Throughout 
the experiment, the hydraulic retention time (HRT) ranged between 8.0 
and 10.0 h, while the aeration rate varied from 4.8 to 7.2 m3/h to ensure 
sufficient DO supply. The AGS-MBR system used simulated wastewater 
as influent and consists of: CH3COONa (769.20 mg/L), NH4Cl (152.67 
mg/L), KH2PO4 (26.36 mg/L), CaCl2 (35.00 mg/L), FeSO4 (0.85–0.95 
mg/L), MgSO4 (20.25 mg/L), NaHCO3 (267.00 mg/L), corresponding 
COD, NH4
+-N and TP concentrations of 600 mg/L, 40 mg/L and 6 mg/L. 
All other aspects related to system configuration and operational con­
ditions remained consistent with the previously reported work (Dai 
et al., 2020).
