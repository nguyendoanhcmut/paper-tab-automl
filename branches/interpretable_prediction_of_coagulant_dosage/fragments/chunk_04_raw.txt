4. Conclusions
In this study, we developed a predictive model for coagulant dosing 
in a water treatment plant using AutoML and interpreted the model 
results to address the transparency issue associated with such predictive 
models. The conclusions are as follows:

performance with a test R2 value of 0.96, RMSE of 0.89, and MAE 
of 0.47. This model offers a faster development cycle and higher 
prediction accuracy compared to manually selected models, and
